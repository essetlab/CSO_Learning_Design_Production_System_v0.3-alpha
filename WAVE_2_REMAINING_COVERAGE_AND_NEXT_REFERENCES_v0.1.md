## Track A — integrated evidence review and readiness gate

One strong reference can combine decision/gate and integrated assessment because both concern the **same evidence bundle and accountable readiness decision**. It should not become GR06 with a different title. GR06 reasons through evidence toward a professional action; Track A evaluates the sufficiency and quality of a learner's work against transparent capability criteria and an explicit assessor/authorization boundary.

Proposed experience: compact evidence dossier, structured criterion-to-evidence mapping, comparison of sufficient/insufficient/conflicting evidence, a gate decision with concise authored rationale, visible remediation route and a bounded reconsideration. Show at least one plausible “not yet” outcome that preserves work and gives a route forward. Do not make another full five-stage text-entry workspace the default. No AI/keyword grading or fixed prepared-answer shortcut may stand in for competence assessment. A deterministic simulated assessor may illustrate a contract, but cannot be represented as an independent real assessment.

Acceptance coverage: evidence provenance and versioning across artifacts; per-criterion sufficiency/limitations; independent assessment semantics; permission/role boundary; partial progress; stale-assessment invalidation; accessible structured alternatives and a full task at native 200%; no gate completion through preview URLs, saved booleans or storage choices. Any reuse of an existing decision/feedback object must be resource-first and preserve its existing consumers.

System Gaps to investigate, not register here: cross-artifact assessment/evidence links, assessment authority/outcome record, revision-dependent reassessment and review/appeal permissions. Reuse neutral UI/evidence primitives where suitable; do not extend SVC-LEARNING-STATE-01 into a credential or scoring authority.

## Track B — practice portfolio, dialogue and consented ecosystem handoff

One **coherent multi-moment lifecycle reference** can cover transfer, portfolio, course/Hub bridge, peer dialogue and contribution: select/prepare a practice artifact → retain a private plan → return with practice evidence → exchange bounded feedback with a distinct reviewer → revise → choose a permissioned handoff/contribution → inspect receipt/moderation/follow-up. These are connected states of one artifact, not five unrelated screens collected under a single number.

The professional capability is carrying a learning output into practice and back into a learning community while preserving evidence, limitations, ownership and consent. The interaction rhythm should emphasize artifact navigation, evidence comparison, response and deliberate handoff, with purposeful short authoring. A portfolio must preserve unresolved work; publishing, receiving a peer reply or gaining moderator approval must not be mandatory proxies for learning completion.

Acceptance coverage: stable artifact identity and version/provenance; private versus shared scope; cross-session return and partial/incomplete evidence; explicit peer and moderator roles; safe/sanitized disclosure choices; independent consent for transfer/contribution; authoritative destination/receipt states, idempotent retry and failed/rejected/pending outcomes; revision after feedback; withdrawal/update handling; accessible mobile/reader/keyboard and storage/network degradation. A download must be labeled a download, not “submitted to Hub.”

System Gaps to investigate: portable artifact envelope/interoperability, permissioned transfer adapter, portfolio index, actor attribution, evidence-dialogue and moderation/handoff state contracts. Local recovery is one dependency, not a collaboration backend. A deterministic mock can prove interface/failure contracts if explicitly labeled; it does **not** prove live Hub integration, authentication or moderation. Gate 8 must specify whether an audited sandbox integration is required to close the bridge claim.

## Why two, and when to split

| Proposed consolidation | Keep combined when… | Split into an additional reference when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate + integrated assessment | One evidence bundle, criteria and accountable readiness decision can demonstrate both without hiding assessment authority | Credentialing/assessor operations introduce a separate permission, assessment or lifecycle contract that cannot be evaluated clearly in the same task |
| Portfolio + bridge + dialogue + contribution | One artifact's private-to-shared lifecycle contains explicit role transitions, retries, consent and response, with separately traceable acceptance criteria | The public/co-creation workflow requires distinct actors/moderation/infrastructure that makes the portfolio task incoherent, or the bridge is only decorative and leaves key boundary tests unproven |

The default proposal is **two additional prototypes**, not a guaranteed maximum. A third focused ecosystem contribution/peer workflow is justified only by the latter split evidence. Do not create separate GR07/08/09/10 prototypes solely to fill numbers. Equally, do not claim fewer prototypes as efficiency if the coverage table's role, permission, semantic or failure-state proof is missing. The current Gate-8 plan owner must map these groupings to accepted reference IDs before freeze.
