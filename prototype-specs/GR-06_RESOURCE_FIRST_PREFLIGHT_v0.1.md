## Professional purpose and difference from earlier references

A CSO project officer must turn mixed monitoring evidence into a proportionate, accountable decision, distinguish findings from explanations, and revise a plan when later evidence challenges the initial interpretation. The retained object is a traceable **evidence-to-decision record**, not a repaired four-section note. GR-03 supplies the distinction between observation and interpretation; GR-05 supplies authored work, honest self-review, immutable history, revision invalidation and shared recovery. Neither existing interaction supports an evidence-batch update with claim-specific support/limitation links and a decision that may legitimately remain unchanged while its rationale and follow-up change.
