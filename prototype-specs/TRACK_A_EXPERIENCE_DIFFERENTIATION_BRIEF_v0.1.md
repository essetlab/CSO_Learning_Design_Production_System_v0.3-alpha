# Track A — Experience Differentiation Brief

**PROPOSED, NOT FROZEN.** Companion JSON conforms to the canonical brief schema; this is not implementation authorization.

## Professional Capability

Review a bounded body of practice work against explicit capability criteria, distinguish sufficient evidence from missing or contradictory evidence, and record an accountable recommendation for the next supervised step with proportionate follow-up.

## Dominant Learner Action

Inspect, map and judge a bounded body of work against criteria; concise writing supports judgment, never dominates.

## Emotional Cognitive Rhythm

Orientation and scan → focused criterion mapping → accountable recommendation → a visible contradiction and consequence → clarification → reconsideration and calm closure. One review page with inline disclosure, no nested stage tabs.

## Visual Interaction Metaphor

A compact review dossier with three criterion rows and a decision memo. Existing shell/tokens; sources are traceable documents, not selectable quiz answer cards.

## Density Scroll Expectation

At most three collapsed criterion summaries, four collapsed source summaries and one decision region on the initial page; only one criterion mapping editor open by default, user may open more. Never duplicate full sources in every row. Max-content tests must still permit full expansion. Initial collapsible outline targets two desktop or four narrow-mobile viewport heights; this is a usability review target, not permission to clip content. 8–12 minute hypothesis; measure at implementation.

## Reference Work Balance

Read mostly supplied work. Source excerpts stay one disclosure away from each criterion; source links return focus to the mapping row. Avoid a permanently expanded long reference column on mobile.

## Authored Writing Burden

Two fields total: rationale ≤45 words and next-step note ≤35 when route/gaps require it; ≤600 characters each; no word minimum. Reuse the same fields during reconsideration; no per-source note, per-criterion reason, four-section repair or separate revision essay. Current record ≤80 words; two-record maximum ≤160 words even if both are wholly rewritten. GR05 has diagnoses, four repaired sections, eight review rounds’ criterion responses and a revision rationale; GR06 has five source notes, two interpretation/limitation pairs, six decision fields and update/review writing. Track A replaces that repeated authoring with three structured mapping rows. Aim 25–50 words per recommendation as optional guidance only.

## Desktop Mobile Transformation

Source summaries beside three criterion rows; expand sources in place. One decision panel below. No kanban, drag wires or multi-column scoring matrix. One-column criterion-first outline with inline source excerpts, source labels/version on every link, open/return preserving focus; comparison pairs stack. No horizontal matrix or nested tab navigation.

## Magnetic Experience Moment

A confident “all activities complete” claim looks ready until its evidence links expose prepared-not-received and unsupported authority. The learner can record not yet without failing, then see exactly what a clarification changes—and what it still does not prove.

## Reuse Rationale

Reuse shell/basic UI and unchanged generic recovery. Harvest immutable version and traceability principles without importing the deep-workspace models. Propose a genuinely typed assessment bundle and readiness interaction, with opt-in scenario/composition variants; no objects registered. Mighty content rows inform scanning only; no vendor CSS or new visual system.

## Difference From Recent References

GR05: author and repair four sections, long studio drafting rhythm, paired artifact comparison. GR06: source-note/interpretation/decision authoring with U01, dense multi-stage workspace; user review sets it as the upper burden bound, not the next template. Track A: compact dossier, three review rows, four supplied artifacts, two concise fields, visible readiness consequence and a short current-version reconsideration. Mobile uses criterion-linked inline evidence rather than nested stage tabs or horizontal matrices. Novelty comes from assessment sufficiency and agency in not yet, not a color/layout variation.
