## Human semantic rubric

No dimension can be established by a nonempty field, link count or keyword. Human reviewer records PASS/DEFICIENCY and a short basis per dimension; reference/fixture review is distinct from assessment of runtime learners.

| ID | Dimension | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Evidence-to-criterion fit | Each link actually supports or limits its own criterion, not a convenient document count. |
| H02 | Contradiction and absence | Unsupported receipt/approval claims and untested access remain visible; missing is not forced into sufficient. |
| H03 | Quality versus completion | Four artifacts, opened sources and valid fields do not establish capability. |
| H04 | Uncertainty and scope | A synthetic rehearsal and clarification support only bounded claims; no population or delivery inference. |
| H05 | Authority | Peer practice, author self-review, tutor review, public decision authority and credentials remain distinct. |
| H06 | Proportionate next step | Remediation is feasible, owned and matched to the gap, with a point for reconsideration. |
| H07 | Version-dependent assessment | New E03 invalidates the current outcome; old reasoning is retained as history, never silently current. |
| H08 | Agency in not yet | Revise, defer and seek review are legitimate; work is preserved and the next route is understandable. |
| H09 | Concise meaningful reasoning | The two brief fields explain enough without filler; structured mappings reduce burden. No generic repeated boilerplate. |
| H10 | Honest completion and differentiation | Practice completion is not competence judgment; no keyword/minimum-word grading; experience is substantially lighter than GR05/06. |

Positive and negative semantic fixtures are in `TRACK_A_SEMANTIC_REVIEW_FIXTURES_v0.1.json`, separate from future runtime inputs. The independent review is planned, not performed.
