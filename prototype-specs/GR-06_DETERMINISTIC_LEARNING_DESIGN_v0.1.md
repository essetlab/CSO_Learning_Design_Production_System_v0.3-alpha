## Capability, authentic problem and retained output

A CSO project officer reviews mixed evidence before the next market consultation round. They must distinguish delivery from influence, account for missing perspectives, recommend a proportionate next step within role boundaries, and revise interpretation and follow-up after a later check. Expected duration 20–30 minutes; introductory HRBA knowledge assumed. Professional certification, legal compliance, statistical causal inference and real-world approval are not assessed.

The Jiru Amba setting is fictional and derived only as context from R10. **Every GR-06 monitoring observation and number is newly proposed synthetic exercise content**, not an event asserted in HRBA R&D or an outcome of GR-05. No GR-05 learner draft is imported. The task starts with conflicting/incomplete evidence rather than a weak document to repair. ## Exact sequence

1. **Evidence:** inspect all five initial records; for each write a brief relevance/limitation note and mark use as informs / limits / both. Seen states orient the learner but never establish quality.
2. **Interpretation:** author one `primary_interpretation` and one `alternative_or_unresolved_interpretation`, each with 1–3 supporting/informing source IDs, 1–3 limiting/counterevidence IDs, a limitation and provisional confidence (low/moderate). The same source may play both roles if the note explains it. The second role can state an unresolved question rather than a competing claim. No equal likelihood, contradictory position or equal confidence is required; evidence may support the primary more strongly. No automatic truth score.
3. **Decision v1:** choose trial adaptation / verify before wider change / retain with monitoring / escalate to seek authorization or system action; cite both interpretation role IDs, explain how the primary and alternative/unresolved interpretation inform the decision and one alternative action, identify responsible role/Awra boundary, action, safe verification and an account-back plan. For escalation, specify what needs authorization/system action and what may proceed while awaiting it; never imply authorization is granted. No route is automatically correct. After structural checks, capture an immutable full v1 and release U01. The choice alone cannot complete the task.
4. **Adaptation:** read U01, write its implication, revise at least one interpretation’s claim or limitation and at least one action/verification/account-back field. Either retain the decision choice with explanation or change it with explanation. Link U01 to at least one interpretation. This ensures an evidence response without forcing an arbitrary decision reversal.
5. **Compare and review:** inspect full v1/current text and links, then answer four criterion-specific prompts with addressed / needs_follow_up, a reason and a next action where needed. Acknowledge only the current reviewed revision. Unresolved follow-ups are permitted and retained. Continue indicates a reviewed training record, not correct professional judgment.
