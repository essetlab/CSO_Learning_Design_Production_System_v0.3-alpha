4. Propose the assessment/gate architecture
Define a deterministic learner/reviewer sequence. At minimum test:
1. inspect a small evidence/artifact bundle;
2. map evidence to capability criteria;
3. identify sufficient / insufficient / conflicting / missing evidence;
4. distinguish evidence quality from simple completion;
5. reach a readiness/gate decision;
6. explain the decision concisely;
7. show consequence/remediation;
8. allow revision or additional evidence;
9. invalidate stale assessment after substantive evidence changes;
10. re-review current evidence before completion.
Do not assume that “proceed” is the correct outcome. At least one legitimate not yet / revise / defer / seek review pathway must be demonstrable without being framed as failure.
