GR-06 · SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXERCISE

Before the next market consultation round in fictional Jiru Amba, review what the first-round records do and do not show. Recommend a feasible step to improve influence and response before priorities are finalized. Later verification will test your reasoning.

G6-E01 | Fictional round-1 facilitation log | One consultation round; attendance only
The log records 40 attendees, including 24 women vendors. It does not record who influenced priorities or who left early.
Limitation: Attendance is not influence; no denominator for all eligible vendors or comparable prior-round count.

G6-E02 | Fictional facilitator summary of six voluntary comments | Six self-selected comments; no population estimate
Four of the six comments mention leaving before priorities were discussed because trading time was affected. The other two comments do not address timing.
Limitation: Do not infer that four out of six represents all vendors, or infer the identities of commenters.

G6-E03 | Fictional pre-meeting access checklist | Venue and materials check only
The entrance was accessible on the day checked. The circulated priority list was available only as a long printed document. No check of alternative formats is recorded.
Limitation: An accessible entrance does not establish usable information or access for every participant.

G6-E04 | Fictional planning-team summary | Eight general suggestions; administrative status
Eight suggestions were logged. Six are marked closed and two pending. The summary does not say whether closed means a decision was explained to the people who raised a suggestion.
Limitation: Closure does not establish response receipt or that a proposal influenced a decision.

G6-E05 | Fictional exercise brief | Boundary for this exercise; not a legal mandate
The next priorities have not yet been finalized. Awra can facilitate input and request explanations; the public planning team makes the planning decisions. No new large meeting is available before finalization.
Limitation: Awra cannot promise a public decision. Proposed alternatives need feasible access and a route into the decision.

TASK: Write a source note for each record (30 words maximum), then a primary interpretation and an alternative or unresolved interpretation. Link informing and limiting evidence; state uncertainty. No equal likelihood is implied.

Choose trial, verify, retain or escalate. Write a reason, alternative, action, responsibility boundary, verification and account-back plan. Preserve this first version before reading the later update.

A later check of the same eight suggestions finds that only two of the six marked closed have a recorded explanation sent back. The other four were closed after internal discussion, without a recorded account-back step. Receipt of the two sent explanations is still unverified.

This clarifies recorded process, not whether people received or understood a response or whether decisions were fair. No identities or complaint details are supplied.

Adapt an interpretation and action, verification or account-back; link U01 and explain the change. The route may stay the same. Compare both versions and review each criterion.
