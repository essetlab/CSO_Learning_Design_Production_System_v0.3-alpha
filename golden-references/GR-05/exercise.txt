Deep Build / Repair Studio — Repair the market participation plan

Awra is supporting consultation on a service-improvement plan in fictional Jiru Amba. Prepare a practical improvement note for the planning team. Focus on women vendors and persons with disabilities, while recognizing that the full case includes other groups. Show who can influence the decision, what access changes are needed, who responds, and what evidence would demonstrate an effect.

ORIGINAL NOTE

Improve market services by holding consultations and installing a feedback box.

Invite everyone to one public meeting to rank the activities already selected by the planning team.

Awra will collect concerns in the feedback box and handle all decisions after the meeting.

Report success using the number of people attending and the number of feedback boxes installed.

EVIDENCE

E01: Women vendors leave discussions early because trading, care and household responsibilities compete with meeting time.

E02: Persons with disabilities face an inaccessible venue and long printed documents.

E03: Participants rank activity cards already chosen by the planning team; transparent stall allocation and influence over market budgets are not available as added priorities.

E04: The plan counts meetings and feedback boxes but does not show whether rights-holders gained influence or received a response.

E05: Awra supports analysis, facilitation, community voice and accountability; it does not replace public authorities.

TASK

Diagnose each original section using 1–3 evidence links. Write four repaired sections; preserve a first version. Review each criterion and give reasons and follow-up actions where needed.

C01: For this exercise, the team cannot add a second large public meeting. Improve your note so affected groups can still influence priorities before the plan is finalized. Keep a feasible response and evidence pathway.

Revise participation plus accountability or evidence. Explain the connected change. Compare both versions and review again. This text fallback does not grant completion.

Q01: Does the objective connect specific rights-holders and barriers to influence or response, rather than only listing activities? Name whose influence changes and the barrier addressed. Holding another meeting is not, by itself, the change sought.

Q02: Can the named groups influence priorities before decisions are finalized, with workable timing and accessible information? Use early accessible information and a feasible small-group or other safe input route. Explain how that input reaches the decision, not just how people attend.

Q03: Is a responsible actor distinct from Awra, with a safe response route and a way to communicate what changed? Do not promise that Awra can make public decisions. Identify the relevant planning or market actor and a non-identifying response process.

Q04: Would the evidence show influence or response without identifying people, and can the proposed team check it? Link a sign of change to a decision or response and an appropriate review point. Counts of attendance or boxes alone are insufficient.
