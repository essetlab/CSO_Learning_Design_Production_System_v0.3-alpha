PRACTITIONER REASONING

Accountability needs a clear owner, a safe route for each concern, action where possible, and communication back. The team should not expose contributors or send a sensitive concern directly to an actor who may be part of the issue.

PRACTICE PRINCIPLE

A feedback mechanism needs a response pathway

Accountability does not end when feedback is collected. Someone must review it, decide the safest and most appropriate route, respond or adapt where possible, and explain back what happened.
