# GR-01 Source & Visual Provenance

## Learning/content sources

The course-entry content is grounded in current HRBA Module 1 source material:

- course title: `Human Rights-Based Approach in CSO Practice`;
- practical orientation for local and grassroots CSOs;
- course purpose framed around everyday HRBA questions;
- source relevance options covering participation/inclusion, advocacy/accountability, stronger project design, evidence/feedback and daily work.

The Wave 1 transformation copy remains prototype content where previously marked as such.

## Visual-source harvest

### Mighty editorial source

Primary source pattern:
**Editorial two column layout**

What was harvested:
- asymmetric dark/light composition;
- large editorial headline against a dark field;
- smaller explanatory field;
- disciplined negative space;
- vertical editorial micro-label;
- publication-like rather than LMS-like tone.

### Mighty illustration source

Secondary source:
**Text with floated images / Illustrated Editorial Article**

What was harvested:
- image integrated into editorial composition rather than placed in a generic card;
- warm human visual tone;
- image/text asymmetry.

### Vertical-text source

Only the restrained vertical editorial marker was harvested.

It is redundant/decorative and disappears on narrower screens.

## Existing HRBA hero reused

The prototype reuses an already-created HRBA Module 1 visual:

`AST-HRBA-GR01-HERO-EXISTING-01.png`

Its original creation brief specified:

- local Ethiopian CSO practitioners;
- the beginning of a learning journey;
- reflection/preparation rather than generic walking;
- notebook and community map;
- Ethiopian highland pathway at sunrise;
- deep navy negative space;
- warm professional tone;
- no logos or political symbols.

This is materially aligned with GR-01's visual purpose.

### Resource-first decision

**REUSE/ADAPT**, not new generation.

A new hero asset was therefore not justified.

## Production ownership

The final prototype does not depend on Mighty at runtime.

Mighty is the visual-pattern source.

The implemented HTML/CSS uses DEC tokens, accessibility behavior, responsive rules and the local Golden Reference architecture.
