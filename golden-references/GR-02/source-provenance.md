# GR-02 Source Provenance

## Source-supported locked content

The existing HRBA Foundations reference defines the Everyday Rights Lens as five questions:

1. Rights & actors — Who holds rights, and who bears duties?
2. Inclusion — Who faces barriers or is missing?
3. Participation — Can people influence decisions safely?
4. Power — What visible, hidden, or invisible power is shaping voice?
5. Accountability — How will information, feedback, response, and correction happen?

The existing Module 2 documentation positions the lens as a synthesis/field-ready checklist before Project Design, rather than a separate graded activity.
