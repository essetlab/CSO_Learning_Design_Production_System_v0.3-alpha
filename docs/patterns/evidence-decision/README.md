# IP-EVIDENCE-DECISION-01

PROTOTYPE only. Evidence to differentiated interpretation to accountable decision to update and review; structural checks never semantic grading.

The learner retains a traceable professional record, not a selected answer. Five stages establish the sequence: inspect and annotate sources; distinguish a primary interpretation from a grounded alternative or unresolved question; author a decision with responsibility and account-back; preserve v1 before releasing later evidence; adapt and compare before criterion review and explicit acknowledgment.

The alternative is not an equal-weight hypothesis by default. Trial, verification, retention and escalation can each be reasoned choices. A later update may justify retaining the same route while changing its interpretation and follow-up. Administrative status, communication sent and confirmed receipt remain distinct.

Structural completion requires authored, bounded fields and typed associations, a preserved first record, a connected update response, current review and acknowledgment. It never establishes semantic quality, statistical attribution, public authority or professional approval. Unresolved follow-up can remain at completion. Any substantive revision invalidates review/acknowledgment while retaining v1.

Reuse requires a governed case contract with source provenance/limitations, differentiated roles, decision/authority fields, staged update timing, field maxima and a separate human semantic rubric. Do not hard-code case answers, require English fluency or add AI/keyword scores. Retention and export remain independent of completion. The generic state service owns storage; the interaction owns learning predicates; the artifact block owns representation; layout variants own arrangement only.
